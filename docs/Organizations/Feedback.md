# Feedback

Use the Feedback page to tell Nodefusion what you think of the portal. It is available at the Read, Write, Manage and Full access levels, but not to the Support user.

## Send a message

1. Under **What would you like to do?**, choose one option:
   - **Give feedback**: tell us about your experience.
   - **Send an idea**: suggest something we could add or improve.
   - **Report a bug**: something in the portal does not work. Describe what you did, what happened and what you expected.
2. Write your text in **Your message**\*. It is required.
3. Select **Send**.

Your browser and portal version are sent with the message.

If the message is empty, a summary at the top of the form says "Fix 1 field to continue." and the field shows "Write a message."

## After sending

The page shows "Thank you, your feedback has been sent". Select **Send another** to write a new message, or **Back to Home** to return to the Home page.

## Notifications

If sending fails, an error message appears. The form stays filled in, so you can select **Send** again.
