# Select an organization

After you sign in, the portal shows the organizations your account can open. Choose one to work in it. For an overview of the screen see [Getting Started](../index.md).

## Your organizations

Each row shows the organization name, your [access level](Organization/Permissions.md#access-levels) as a chip and, where it applies, a **Customer agreement required** tag.

- Select a row to open the Home page of that organization.
- If the row has the **Customer agreement required** tag, the organization does not open. The customer agreement dialog appears instead, see [Customer Agreement](../Customer-Agreement.md).
- A search field (**Search organizations**) appears only when you have access to 6 or more organizations. It filters the list by name.

## Don't see your organization?

The bar **Don't see your organization?** offers two forms. Select **Request access** or **Activate token** to open one; select the same button again to close it.

### Request access

Use it to ask for access to an organization that is not in your list.

1. Fill in **Organization name\*** and **VAT ID\***.
2. Select **Send access request**.

The request is sent to Nodefusion and is not carried out immediately. You receive an email with an access token.

### Activate token

Use it to add an organization to your account with an access token you received by email (after a request to Nodefusion or from an invitation by a user with Full access).

1. Paste the token into **Token\***.
2. Select **Activate**.

After a successful activation the page shows the message "Permissions granted. Please logout and login again." and you are signed out automatically after 10 seconds. Sign in again and the organization appears in the list.

If you open the link from the email, the page is titled **Activate Organization Token** and the form is already open with the token filled in. Only select **Activate**. The token works only for the person whose email the invitation was sent to.

## Register a company

The card **Register another company** opens the customer registration in a new browser tab, so you can add a new company as a Nodefusion customer.

If your account has no organizations yet, the page says so and shows the card **New to Nodefusion?** (register your company as a customer), followed by the bar with the **Request access** and **Activate token** forms.

## Notifications

- After **Send access request**: the message "Access request response received." appears. You do not have access yet: Nodefusion handles the request first and you then receive an email with an access token. The form stays open.
- After a successful **Activate**: the page message described above.
- If sending or activation fails, an error message appears. The form keeps what you entered, so you can try again. See [Getting Started](../index.md#messages-and-notifications).
